OEE, scrap rate, first-pass yield, downtime, on-time delivery, safety incidents, and units produced per hour are already tracked across plants, production lines, and shifts. So when leadership introduces Objectives and Key Results (OKRs), plant managers may reasonably ask: Aren’t we already doing this?
